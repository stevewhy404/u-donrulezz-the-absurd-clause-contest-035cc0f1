One original English sentence, at most 200 characters, phrased as an absurd
clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement: deadpan legal
boilerplate that quietly turns absurd.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes the clause to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> Any soul not reclaimed within thirty (30) days of termination shall be
> deemed abandoned property and resold as refurbished, with no warranty,
> express or implied.
